Help & support

Recipe Box keeps every recipe you love in one notebook: recipes shared from videos and websites, pages scanned from your cookbooks, and the ones you write yourself.

Frequently asked questions

How do I save a recipe from a video or a website?

In TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or Safari, tap Share, then Recipe Box. You can also copy the link, tap + in the app, then Paste a link. Recipe Box reads the public page and pulls out the ingredients and steps it finds. When a video only gives the recipe out loud or by private message, the app keeps the title, photo and caption, and you can write the rest.

How do I scan a cookbook page?

Tap +, then Scan a page, and point the camera at the recipe. The text is read on your iPhone; the photo is never uploaded. Check the ingredients and steps, then save.

What is free, and what does Pro unlock?

The free version includes 3 imports in total (link, scan or catalog recipe). Reading your notebook and writing recipes by hand stay unlimited, and imported recipes stay yours forever. Pro unlocks unlimited imports and the grocery list sorted by aisle, with a yearly subscription (free trial for new subscribers) or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Recipe Box, go to Settings, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Recipe Box, open Settings and tap Manage subscription. Or, on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Recipe Box. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next year.

Where are my recipes stored?

On your iPhone only. There is no account and no cloud; your recipes open without a connection. Deleting the app deletes them.

Where do the catalog recipes come from?

From the Wikibooks Cookbook, written by its contributors and shared under the CC BY-SA 4.0 license. Each recipe shows its source and the author and license of its photo.
