Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short: your recipes stay on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Who we are

Recipe Box is published by Abdelilah Cherkaoui. Contact: cherkaoui.abdo@gmail.com.

What the app does with your data

  • Your recipes. Recipes, notes, collections and your grocery list are stored on your iPhone. They are deleted when you delete the app. We have no server and never receive them.
  • Imported links. When you import a link, your iPhone reads that public page directly, as Safari would. Nothing goes through us.
  • Scanned pages. The text of a photographed page is read on your iPhone with Apple's on-device tools. No image is uploaded.
  • Catalog photos. Photos of catalog recipes load from Wikimedia Commons (or from our own static hosting for retouched photos), like any web page: those servers see your iPhone's IP address, nothing else.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your name or payment details.

What we don't do

No account, no advertising, no ad tracking, no analytics. We neither sell nor share any data.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For any question, write to cherkaoui.abdo@gmail.com. You can also contact your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from them.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with its date.
